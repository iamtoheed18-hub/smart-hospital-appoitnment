Who We Are

A Legacy of Healing & Compassion

Founded in 1995, MedCare Hospital has grown from a small clinic to one of India's premier multi-specialty hospitals. With over 500 beds, 100+ doctors, and 20+ specialized departments, we deliver world-class healthcare to over 50,000 patients annually.

Our mission is simple: to provide the highest quality medical care with compassion, integrity, and innovation. Every patient who walks through our doors is treated like family.
